The problem

Cloud migrations fail not because the tech is hard, but because the process is invisible.

01

No plan, just chaos

Migrations are stitched together with scripts, spreadsheets, and Slack threads. No single view of what's in-flight, what's failed, or what comes next.

02

Cutover anxiety

Teams have no automated rollback plan, no pre-checks, and no automated validation. Every cutover is a manual, high-stress event.

03

No post-migration proof

Row counts are checked manually or not at all. Data drift goes undetected for weeks. No checksum comparison, no sample diff.

DClaw Migrate puts structure and AI at every step — from schema discovery to post-cutover optimization.

Schema Discovery

Connect. Introspect. Map.

01

User clicks Discover Schema on Job Detail → Schema Mapper tab

02

POST /api/v1/jobs/{id}/discover — backend connects to source DB via asyncpg

03

Introspects information_schema.tables + columns, row counts

04

Draft SchemaMapping rows created. User edits transform rules, excludes tables.

⚠️

Supported source types: postgresql · aws_rds · gcp_cloudsql · azure_sql. MySQL/MongoDB: roadmap.

DClaw Migrate 10 / 14
Wave planning

Migrate 1,000 assets safely — in the right order.

Waves group jobs into ordered, dependency-aware batches. Sequence 1 completes before Sequence 2 begins. AI suggests grouping based on risk and dependencies.

WAVE ENTITY
name · sequence_order
status: planned → in_progress → completed
risk_level: low · medium · high
jobs[] → list[MigrationJob]
EXAMPLE: 3-WAVE MIGRATION PLAN
1
Non-critical services — reporting, analytics, dev tools
Low blast radius · Risk: Low · Sequence first to build confidence
planned
2
Core application databases — read replicas first, then primaries
Moderate blast radius · Risk: Medium · Requires Wave 1 completion
planned
3
Production auth, payments, and customer-facing APIs
High blast radius · Risk: High · Full validation + cutover rehearsal required
planned

AI Wave Planner: POST /api/v1/ai/plan-waves — pass your job list; AI returns groupings with rationale and recommended sequence order.

Cutover management

Execute with confidence.
Roll back in one click.

Every job gets a dedicated CutoverPlan with strategy selection, pre/post checklists, and rollback procedure. The state machine enforces sequencing — you can't mark complete without executing first.

planned → executing → completed
executing → rolled_back POST /cutover/rollback

Strategy
blue-green · direct · phased
Pre-checks
Checklist JSON — verified before execute
Post-checks
Checklist JSON — verified after execute

Step-by-step text — AI-generated or manual

POST /ai/plan-cutover — AI generates the full checklist and timing recommendations based on job status, validation results, and migration type.

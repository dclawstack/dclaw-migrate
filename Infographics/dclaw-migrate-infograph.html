The platform

One platform.
The full migration lifecycle.

DClaw Migrate replaces the patchwork of migration scripts, spreadsheets, and Slack threads with a structured, AI-assisted workflow. Every migration lives in one place: connections, wave plans, schema mappings, execution logs, validation reports, cutover checklists, and AI-generated runbooks.

The AI copilot is context-aware — it knows your source/target connection types, your schema, and your current job state. Ask it to assess risk, suggest column mappings, draft a rollback procedure, or explain a validation failure.

🗺️

Wave-based migration planning

Group jobs into ordered waves. AI optimizes sequencing and balances risk across batches.

🔍

Live schema discovery

Connect to source PostgreSQL-family DB, introspect tables and columns, generate draft mappings automatically.

✅

Post-migration validation

Per-table row count comparison, checksum verification, and sample diff. Pass/fail per table.

🔄

Blue-green cutover with 1-click rollback

Execute cutover, mark complete, or roll back with a single API call. Pre/post check checklists included.
